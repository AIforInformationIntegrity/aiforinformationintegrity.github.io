The AI4 Institute works at the intersection of artificial intelligence and social good, with a focus on information integrity and media literacy. Inspired by Mila’s mission, we develop research and tools that strengthen the information ecosystem, empower individuals to critically engage with media, and contribute to a more trustworthy and democratic digital society.
